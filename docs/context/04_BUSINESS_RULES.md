Core Rules

- Every enquiry creates exactly one Lead.
- Every Lead has one active admission journey.
- Lead may have multiple interested courses.
- Store every message.
- Store every timeline event.
- Timeline is immutable.
- Every scheduled future action is a Promise.
- Promises execute once.
- Promises can be cancelled.
- Admission creates Student while preserving Lead.
- Lead is never hard deleted.
- Documents sent are logged.
- Templates are editable.
- Automation rules are stored in database.
